The Vision

Photography Rooted
in Montana

Based in Bozeman, Montana, Ash Juric Photography captures the texture of this place — rugged landscapes, real people, and the quiet drama that lives between fashion and wilderness.

The primary focus is people and commercial work — editorial shoots, brand campaigns, and portrait sessions that feel honest and visually striking. Every session is shaped around the person, the light, and the land.

Wildlife photography is a personal passion — the kind that pulls you out before sunrise and asks you to be still. Wolves, grizzlies, bison — Montana never stops surprising.
